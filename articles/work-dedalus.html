AI Engineer, Dedalus

AI Engineer at Dedalus since July 2022, working remotely

I joined Dedalus in July 2022, and I work there remotely as an AI Engineer in the Innovation & Emerging Technologies Office of Dedalus Italia. My work covers assistants that retrieve information before they answer, AI agents, and what it takes to run them.

Lab test mapping

Clinical labs name their tests in their own way, and each name has to be mapped to LOINC, a shared standard. Doing this by hand is slow and error-prone. I built LARA with CNR-IIT. It is an assistant that suggests LOINC codes for local lab test names in more than one language, while the people doing the mapping make the decisions.

I presented it at the LOINC Conference in Atlanta in 2023 and at AIME 2025 in Pavia. In 2026 I spoke at a round table on AI for lab test mapping at the LOINC Conference in Cologne. The work has also been published as a book chapter, and LARA won a prize at the Como Lake Awards.

Assistants, agents and infrastructure

I have also developed an internal enterprise chatbot that uses retrieval-augmented generation: it looks up relevant material first, then answers from it. My work on it includes a backend, a front-end widget and pages. In May 2026, at Cloud-Native Days Italy in Bologna, a colleague and I gave a talk on the anatomy of an enterprise chatbot, covering architecture, security, governance and integration.

Another part of my job is building agentic AI tools, both for software development and for daily company tasks. I also work on the infrastructure around these projects, including how they are tested, delivered and kept secure. A useful assistant needs that groundwork as much as it needs a good model.
