Machine Learning Engineer, AAK TELE-SCIENCE, INC.

Remote work on Deep Learning and NLP models for matching investors with researchers, April 2021 to March 2022

From April 2021 to March 2022 I worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at AAK Tele-Science, Inc., a company in Davis, California. I worked remotely.

The problem

Investors and researchers were hard to match. My work there was to develop Deep Learning and NLP models that could help connect the two.

NLP, or natural language processing, is the part of AI that works with written language. It mattered here because part of what we can learn about researchers is written down in what they publish.

Two kinds of clues

The matching drew on two sources: investment history and scientific publications. One says something about where money has gone before. The other says something about what research has been about.

These are very different kinds of information. The idea, in plain words, was to use models to learn from both, so that a match could rest on what investors had actually funded and what had actually been published.

What I can share

I am keeping this page short and general on purpose. I will not go into how the company's systems work, and I am not giving figures about the results. What I can say is what the job was about: using Deep Learning and language to help investors and researchers find each other.
