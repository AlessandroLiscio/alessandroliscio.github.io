LARA at the LOINC Conference in Atlanta

On Thursday 19 October 2023, at the LOINC Conference in Atlanta, I presented LARA. LARA was developed by Dedalus together with CNR-LOINC Italia, and its goal is better semantic interoperability in healthcare data: making the standardisation of laboratory data simpler.

The problem

Clinical laboratories use their own local names for tests. Each name has to be mapped to a LOINC code. Done by hand, this is slow and error-prone.

What LARA does

LARA is an AI assistant that suggests LOINC codes for local laboratory test names, in more than one language. The people doing the mapping review the suggestions and decide.

The occasion

I built LARA with CNR-IIT, and Atlanta, the conference of the standard it serves, was where Dedalus unveiled it.
